Dutch vocabulary list from handwritten photos
English translations and example sentences are supplied for study purposes. Categories are suggested from the word's grammatical role and meaning; you can refine them in your app.

Important notes
- — means not normally applicable or no plural is used in the given sense.
- Verb forms are present singular (third person: hij/zij/het), past singular, and past participle.
- Some English prompts are ambiguous. For example, to mean can be menen (“I mean it”) or betekenen (“what does this word mean?”).
- A few handwritten entries may have been intended differently; check the review notes at the end.
- Articles are included for nouns. For verbs, adverbs and adjectives, article is —.
