## Prepositions

| Dutch word | English translation | Category | Example (Dutch) | Example (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voor | before; in front of; for | prepositions | Ik wacht voor de deur. | I am waiting in front of the door. |
| Achter | behind | prepositions | De fiets staat achter het huis. | The bicycle is behind the house. |
| Boven | above; upstairs; on top of | prepositions | De lamp hangt boven de tafel. | The lamp hangs above the table. |
| Onder | under; below | prepositions | De kat ligt onder de stoel. | The cat is under the chair. |
| Bij | at; near; with | prepositions | Ik wacht bij de ingang. | I am waiting near the entrance. |
| In | in; inside | prepositions | De sleutels liggen in de tas. | The keys are in the bag. |
| Tussen | between | prepositions | De bank staat tussen de twee stoelen. | The sofa is between the two chairs. |
| Tegenover | opposite; across from | prepositions | De apotheek is tegenover de supermarkt. | The pharmacy is opposite the supermarket. |
| Met | with; by (means of) | prepositions | Ik ga met de bus naar mijn werk. | I go to work by bus. |
| Tegen | against; to; towards | prepositions | De fiets staat tegen de muur. | The bicycle is against the wall. |
| Naast | next to; beside | prepositions | De bakker is naast de bank. | The bakery is next to the bank. |
| Op | on; onto; at | prepositions | Het boek ligt op tafel. | The book is on the table. |
| Aan | on; at; attached to | prepositions | De jas hangt aan de kapstok. | The coat hangs on the coat rack. |
| Om | around; at (time); for | prepositions | We wandelen om het park. | We walk around the park. |

## Review notes

| Dutch word | Content note |
|---|---|
| Dicht | Can mean “closed” or “near,” depending on context. |
| Licht | Can mean “lightweight” or “bright”; the example uses “lightweight.” |
| Hard | Can mean “hard,” “loud,” or “fast,” depending on context. |
| Veel / Weinig | These words often function as quantity words rather than typical adjectives, but they are kept in the requested adjectives section. |
| Hetzelfde / Anders | These can function as pronouns/adverbs as well as describe a quality; they are kept in the requested adjectives section. |
| Voor / Om / Tegen / Bij / Aan / Op | These prepositions have several meanings depending on context; the examples show one common use each. |
