## Review notes

| Dutch verb | Content note |
|---|---|
| Uitsappen | Unclear as written; may be a typo for **uitslapen** (“to sleep in”). Confirm the intended verb before adding it to the learning system. |
| Wegbrengen | The example uses the more natural phrasing **Ik breng mijn dochter naar school**; the separable particle is not included in that sentence because the destination phrase is used. |
| Ingaan | Meaning depends on context; for rules or agreements, **ingaan** can mean “to take effect.” |
| Afgaan | Meaning depends on context, including an alarm going off or a person failing/being embarrassed. |
